What this MVP does

  1. Captures name, phone, pickup location, vehicle, need.
  2. Opens a text to the driver’s cell with that lead (your SMS, not Twilio).
  3. Driver accepts / declines by replying on that thread (or the reply buttons).
  4. Keeps a minimal booked-job log on this device.

What it cannot do without Twilio / paid telephony: answer the driver’s real ring automatically, forward missed calls, or send SMS from a system number while the caller stays silent.
